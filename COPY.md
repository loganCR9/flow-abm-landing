# Turn target accounts into sales conversations

Flow ABM nurtures buying groups and gives sales the evidence to start the right conversation.

[See it in action](https://flow-abm-demo.pages.dev/demo)
[How it works](#how-it-works)

## How it works

### Approve the account play
Turn your brief into a plan and content, then approve the content and target list.

### Nurture the buying group
Launch coordinated touches that speak to each stakeholder’s role.

### Give sales the evidence
Review account readiness and approve the handoff for sales to accept or recycle.

## Know when sales should act

### Plans you approve
Turn approved briefs into plans and content your team reviews before launch.

### Reach each buying role
Keep stakeholders engaged with role-specific nurture and frequency caps.

### Explain every handoff
See who engaged, what happened, and why the account is ready.

### Count real engagement
Readiness ignores email opens and bot clicks, including security scanners.

### Keep humans in control
People approve each handoff; sales accepts or recycles it with a reason.

### Respect every opt-out
Built-in suppression and opt-out checks run before every touch.
